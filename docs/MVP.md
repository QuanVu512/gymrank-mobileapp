# MVP - GymRank

## Muc tieu san pham

Tao mot app tap luyen don gian, de dung, co dong luc ro rang:

- Nguoi moi biet nen tap gi.
- Nguoi tap gym ghi lai set, rep, muc ta.
- Nguoi cardio/the thao van duoc cong EXP va len level.
- Nguoi tap tang co duoc cong Rank Point theo nhom co.
- Bodygraph cho thay nhom co nao dang duoc chu trong hoac bi bo quen.

## Pham vi ban dau

### Co trong MVP

- Onboarding ngan: ten, kinh nghiem, muc tieu, gioi tinh, so ngay co the tap/tuang.
- Home: Level, EXP, Streak, lich hom nay.
- Routine: tao lich tap theo thu trong tuan.
- Beginner auto plan: goi y lich an toan cho nguoi moi.
- Workout tracker: bai tap, set, rep, weight, thoi luong.
- Exercise library: 30-60 bai dau tien.
- Scoring: EXP, Rank Point, Muscle Rank.
- Streak: 1 tuan du 3 buoi thi giu chuoi.
- Bodygraph: to mau nhom co theo rank/cham soc.
- Progress chart co ban.

### De sau MVP

- Ban be, feed xa hoi.
- Global leaderboard.
- AI coach nang cao.
- Camera check dong tac.
- Thu vien 500+ bai tap.
- Publish Google Play.
